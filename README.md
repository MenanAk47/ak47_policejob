## 🔥 Key Features

This resource is packed with modules to enhance every aspect of law enforcement roleplay:

### 👮‍♂️ Advanced Interactions & Actions
* **Tactical Cuffing**: Front and back cuffing animations with resistance minigames.
* **Dynamic Escorting**: Drag suspects, put them in vehicles, or even throw them in the **trunk**.
* **Tackle System**: Sprint and tackle fleeing suspects to the ground.
* **Blindfolds & Ropes**: Restrict suspect vision and movement for high-stakes RP.

### ⚖️ Jail & Community Service
* **Cinematic Jail Intro**: Immersive cutscenes when processing prisoners.
* **Prison Economy**: Prisoners must complete jobs (e.g., sweeping, electrical work) to reduce sentences.
* **Jailbreak System**: Interactive breakout mechanics requiring tools (shovels, acid) and minigames to cut fences or dig tunnels.
* **Community Service**: Alternative punishment system where players must complete public works tasks to gain freedom.

### 🕵️‍♂️ Evidence & Investigation
* **Crime Scene Trace**: Bullet shells, blood drops, and fingerprints persist in the world.
* **Forensics Tools**: Use **UV Lights** to find fingerprints and **GSR Tests** to detect gunshot residue on suspects.
* **Evidence Management**: Collect, analyze, and store evidence in a dedicated police database.

### 🚔 Traffic & Vehicle Policing
* **ANPR / Flagged Plates**: Automatic number plate recognition with alerts for flagged vehicles.
* **GPS Trackers**: Discreetly attach trackers to suspect vehicles and monitor their location.
* **Impound & Seize**: Robust system to impound, depot, or permanently seize vehicles.
* **Spikestrips & Objects**: Deploy cones, barriers, and functional spikestrips to control traffic flow.

### 🛠️ Additional Modules
* **Ankle Monitors**: Enforce house arrest with designated safe zones and alert triggers.
* **Security Cameras**: Access and control CCTV cameras across the map (banks, stores, etc.).
* **Police Garage**: Integrated vehicle spawner with livery/extra customization and repair stations.
* **Helicam**: Advanced helicopter camera with spotlight, rappel, and thermal vision support.
* **Metal Detectors**: Placeable scanners that detect weapons and illegal items.
